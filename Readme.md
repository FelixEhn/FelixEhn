### About me

I'm a dual student from Germany studying **Business Informatics (Wirtschaftsinformatik)** at DHBW Mosbach, specializing in **Application Management** with a focus on **Applied Artificial Intelligence**.

Alternating between university and practical phases lets me apply theory directly in real-world projects. In my free time I love building software for live events — and I work hands-on in event technology myself. I also volunteer with the **THW** (German Federal Agency for Technical Relief).

- 🎓 &nbsp;B.Sc. Business Informatics — DHBW Mosbach
- 💻 &nbsp;Software development, IT systems & the intersection of tech and business
- 🎛️ &nbsp;Software for live events — show control, timecode & remote tools
- 🎚️ &nbsp;Event technology — lighting, audio & stage
- 🛟 &nbsp;Volunteer at the THW (Technisches Hilfswerk)
